About the Safari Rally

The safari Rally is one the toughtest motosport events in the world, known for its rugged terrain and unpredictable weather conditions. Set in the heart of Kenya, this rally tests the endurance of both drivers and machines.

Why Kenya?

Kenya's landscapes, from the savannahs to the mountains, provide the perfect backdrop for the epic rally. The safari Rally brings together adventure, nature, and motorsports in a unique blend.
